Drip campaigns help ecommerce brands move beyond one-off email blasts by automatically delivering relevant messages at the right time, turning customer actions into ongoing revenue. That’s where the revenue impact comes from. Omnisend’s 2026 ecommerce marketing report found that automated messages were just 2% of email sends in 2025.
