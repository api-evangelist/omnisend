A Black Friday checklist helps you prepare your store, marketing, offers, and operations before Black Friday/Cyber Monday traffic peaks. Follow it early, and you’ll avoid last-minute problems while giving your store the best chance to maximize holiday sales. In 2026, BFCM runs from Friday, November 27 to Monday, November 30.
