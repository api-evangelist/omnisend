Order confirmation SMS are automated transactional messages sent immediately after purchase, containing details like order numbers and tracking links. The article provides seven scenario-based examples (standard, first-time buyer, personalized, upsell/cross-sell, express delivery, high-value, and subscription confirmations) along with copy-ready templates. Success depends on concise messaging under 160 characters, personalization, immediate delivery timing, and clear calls-to-action.
