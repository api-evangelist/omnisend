Lifecycle email marketing sends targeted messages based on where customers are in their relationship with a brand, using automation and behavioral triggers. Unlike traditional campaigns, it segments subscribers across acquisition, conversion, retention, and reactivation stages. Key automated flows include welcome series, onboarding emails, retention campaigns, and win-back sequences designed to move customers through their journey and maximize revenue.
