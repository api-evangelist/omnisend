Email deliverability requires proper authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), list hygiene, and strong engagement signals to ensure messages reach the primary inbox rather than spam folders. The guide outlines 12 strategies for ecommerce brands including domain warming, double opt-in verification, regular list cleaning, audience segmentation, and monitoring spam complaint rates and bounce rates. Success depends on balancing sender reputation management with subscriber engagement and content quality.
